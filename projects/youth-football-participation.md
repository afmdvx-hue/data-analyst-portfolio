## Context

As Vice-Chairperson at Tiger Bay Youth Development in Butetown, Cardiff, I saw an opportunity to use data to improve how we plan sessions, allocate coaches, and measure the impact of our discipline and inclusion work.

## What I did

- Collected attendance and age-group data across weekly sessions
- Logged pitch misconduct incidents before and after culture initiatives
- Built summary views in Google Sheets and explored visualisations in Tableau
- Identified which age groups had the highest drop-off and where extra coach support was needed

## Insights

- Consistent attendance tracking made it easier to spot players at risk of leaving the programme
- Behavioural incidents fell as we paired data review with structured conversations involving parents and schools
- Former players returning as volunteer coaches became a measurable pipeline when we tracked participation over time

## Skills demonstrated

Data compilation, stakeholder communication, visualisation, and turning operational records into decisions that support a community organisation.

## Objective

Apply the Google Data Analytics framework — Ask, Prepare, Process, Analyse, Share, Act — to a live analytics dataset and produce a report suitable for non-technical stakeholders.

## Analysis

- Reviewed sessions, bounce rate, and average session duration by landing page
- Compared acquisition channels (organic, direct, referral, social)
- Identified top-performing content and pages with high exit rates
- Summarised findings in Google Sheets with supporting charts

## Deliverable

A concise stakeholder report highlighting where traffic was coming from, which pages engaged users, and recommended next steps for content or campaign focus.

## Relevance

Shows my ability to work with web analytics tools, compile metrics into a clear narrative, and communicate findings to decision-makers.
